### Character Sprite Artist Specification (Handoff Guide)
Teammates wishing to swap in hand-drawn art can use Aseprite, Piskel, or Lospec Pixel Editor:
- **Canvas Dimensions**: 32×32 pixels per frame
- **Target Sprite A**: `cerberus` (3-headed cyber-hound mascot of FTC #31113; 2 running stride frames)
- **Target Sprite B**: `mini-robot` (Miniature DecodeBot companion robot with mecanum base and intake)
- **Export Formats**:
  1. Direct character matrix update in `web/src/components/pixel/pixel-sprites.ts` (32 lines × 32 chars)
  2. Or static 32×32 PNG placed into `web/public/pixel/` and imported into `PixelSprite.tsx`
